"The YouTube Sentiment Analysis system analyzes user comments and classifies them as Positive, Negative, or Neutral. First, comments are collected and preprocessed. Then TF-IDF converts text into numerical vectors. A Logistic Regression model is trained using labeled comments. The trained model is integrated with a Flask web application where users can enter comments and instantly receive sentiment predictions."

Advantages

Analyzes audience feedback automatically.
Fast sentiment detection.
Useful for content creators and businesses.

Disadvantages

May not understand sarcasm.
Accuracy depends on dataset quality.
Complex language can cause misclassification.
